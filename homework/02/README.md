# Homework 02 – User Research, Persona, and User Journey

For this homework, I explored how exotic pet keepers manage feeding, shedding, and environmental information, focusing on record-keeping habits and the difficulties of maintaining and retrieving care records.

## Research Activities

The work includes:

- A research plan with two research questions and three participant profiles.
- Three AI-simulated interviews representing informal, methodical, and technology-oriented keepers.
- An affinity map identifying four patterns across the simulated interviews.
- Two provisional research insights about recording effort and fragmented care information.
- A primary UX persona based on the simulated research.
- A user journey showing an informal keeper attempting to find a gecko's previous shedding date.

The simulations were informed by published research on reptile keeping and pet-tracking technology. They are exploratory scenarios, not interviews with real participants.

## Main Findings

The simulations suggest two possible design priorities:

1. Making routine care records quick and easy to enter.
2. Keeping animal care history and environmental information accessible together.

These findings remain provisional and would require validation with real users.
