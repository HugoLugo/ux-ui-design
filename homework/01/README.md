# Homework 01 – Finding an Idea and Exploring the Problem

For this homework, I created a FigJam board for my individual UX/UI Design project, Exotic Pet Keeper.

The project explores the difficulties exotic pet keepers may experience when organizing and accessing information about their animals, including feeding, shedding, temperature, and humidity.

The board includes:
- The problem and target users.
- User and organizational goals.
- The main constraint.
- A measurable success criterion.
- Assumptions, personal observations, and open questions.
- The riskiest assumption and an initial project pitch.

The main assumption to investigate is whether easy access to digital records would encourage exotic pet keepers to record their animals' care regularly.
